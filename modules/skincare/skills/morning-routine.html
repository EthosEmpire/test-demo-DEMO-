Morning Routine

Skill · 4 min · Skincare Module

The morning routine sets up your skin's protection for the day. It should take no longer than 5 minutes and focuses on cleansing, hydrating, and shielding from UV and environmental damage.

The Steps (in order)

1
Cleanse

Use a gentle, non-comedogenic cleanser. Wet your face with lukewarm water, apply a small amount, massage for 30–60 seconds, rinse thoroughly. Pat dry — do not rub.

Skip the cleanser and just rinse with water in the morning if your skin is very dry or sensitive. Cleansing is more important at night.

2
Toner (optional)

Apply toner to a cotton pad or directly to your hands and gently press into skin. Toners rebalance the skin's pH after cleansing. If your cleanser is gentle, toner is not essential.

3
Serum (if using one)

Apply serum before moisturizer. A few drops, pressed gently into skin. Common morning serums: hyaluronic acid (hydration) or Vitamin C (brightening, antioxidant protection).

4
Moisturizer

Apply a small amount and spread evenly across face and neck. Use a lightweight gel for oily skin, a lotion or cream for dry skin. Allow to absorb for 1–2 minutes before SPF.

5
SPF — Always Last

Apply SPF 30 or higher as the final step, every single morning. Use about a quarter teaspoon (a nickel-sized amount) for the face. Do not forget the ears and back of the neck.

Mineral sunscreens (zinc oxide, titanium dioxide) are better tolerated by sensitive and acne-prone skin. Chemical sunscreens are thinner and easier to layer under makeup.

Common Mistakes

  • Applying SPF but rubbing it in until it disappears — you may be removing it. Press gently.
  • Skipping SPF because it's cloudy — UVA rays penetrate clouds.
  • Using too many products in the morning — the morning routine should be minimal. Save treatments for night.
  • Not waiting between layers — if possible, wait 30–60 seconds between steps to allow absorption.
Practice Task: Time your morning routine tomorrow. It should be under 5 minutes. If it takes longer, identify what to cut.
