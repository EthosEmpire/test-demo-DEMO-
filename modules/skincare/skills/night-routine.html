Night Routine

Skill · 4 min · Skincare Module

The night routine is when real skin repair happens. During sleep, cell turnover increases, skin produces more collagen, and your products can work without UV interference. This is when you treat, not just protect.

The Steps (in order)

1
Remove SPF and Makeup First

If you wore SPF (you did), start with a cleansing oil or micellar water to dissolve it. SPF forms a layer that a regular cleanser alone cannot fully remove. This is called double cleansing.

If you wear no makeup and use a mineral sunscreen, a single gentle cleanser is usually enough. Double cleansing is most important if you wore chemical SPF or foundation.

2
Cleanse

Follow with your regular gentle cleanser. This second cleanse removes the oil cleanser residue, remaining pollution, and the day's sweat. Take your time — 60 to 90 seconds. Rinse with lukewarm water. Pat dry.

3
Toner (optional)

Same as morning. Optional but useful if your skin is congested or you are using AHA/BHA treatments.

4
Treatment (2–3x per week only)

This is where active ingredients go. Choose ONE and use it 2 to 3 nights per week, not every night.

  • Retinol — start once per week, increase slowly over months. Do not use with BHAs.
  • BHA (Salicylic acid) — for acne-prone or congested pores. Use 2–3x per week.
  • AHA (Glycolic or Lactic acid) — for texture, dark spots, dullness. Use 2–3x per week.

Never use AHA and BHA together, and never combine retinol with either acid. Pick one treatment to focus on for 30 days before evaluating.

5
Moisturizer

At night, you can use a richer moisturizer than in the morning. Look for ceramides, hyaluronic acid, or glycerin. Apply while skin is still slightly damp from toner or treatment — this locks in more moisture.

6
Sleeping Habits That Affect Skin

Your pillowcase directly contacts your face for 6–8 hours. Change it at least twice per week. Use a silk or satin pillowcase if you can — it causes less friction and moisture absorption than cotton.

Do not sleep with hair on your face if it is oily or has product in it.

The On/Off Schedule

A simple rotation for nights using treatments:

  • Monday: Cleanser → Moisturizer (recovery night)
  • Tuesday: Cleanser → BHA → Moisturizer
  • Wednesday: Cleanser → Moisturizer
  • Thursday: Cleanser → Retinol → Moisturizer
  • Friday: Cleanser → Moisturizer
  • Saturday: Cleanser → BHA → Moisturizer
  • Sunday: Cleanser → Moisturizer (recovery)
Practice Task: Run a proper double cleanse tonight if you have not done it before. Notice whether your skin feels different than a single-cleanse night.
